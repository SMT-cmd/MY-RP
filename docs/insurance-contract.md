# Local incidents and insurance contract

Components of R034–R036 and R040, with parts of A11 exercised locally. No complete requirement, full acceptance or public release gate is marked passed. D12 remains a proposed balance gate; numbers below are authored development seeds.

An incident comes from server-recorded exposure, never a submitted claim amount. Low vehicle condition increases a bounded breakdown chance during a real funded journey. Neglected home fire safety increases a bounded incident chance during actual metered utility use. Each asset has at most one exposure per day; losses are bounded and game health retains a starter or ordinary safety floor. Existing journeys still reach a safe destination. Neither an incident nor a risk flag debits a personal wallet or creates a conviction.

Incident core evidence is immutable. A completed actual asset repair can attach one paid invoice with an immutable command receipt. Clinical intake links the most recent recorded health exposure so buying coverage after an injury cannot reclassify its onset. Claim evidence uses owned asset records, real incidents, completed clinical episodes and paid invoices. Amounts, severity and diagnoses submitted by a client have no authority.

A policy costs 5,000 minor units, covers at most 50,000 and has a 5,000 excess, one-day waiting period and thirty-day duration. It covers nonroutine game clinical expenses or specified owned vehicle/property repairs. Routine elective care and preexisting exposure are excluded. Claims must be filed within seven days of exposure. Asset claims cannot exceed both recorded damage and the verified repair fee, minus excess and prior reimbursement. Clinical claims use actually settled consultation and treatment costs. Each expense reference gets one claim and one independent appeal within seven days of denial.

Every policy reserves 100% of its entire maximum liability at sale. A player insurer uses real company capital; the NPC insurer uses a named backing source capped at 1,000,000 minor units per day. Premium stays reserved and refundable during the first day before a claim. Earned premium and unused expired backing are separate transfers. Insurers cannot spend customer coverage reserves through company roles.

Claims progress through submission, scoped evidence requests, evidence investigation, factual approval or denial, reserved payout and payment. Approval transfers coverage backing into a dedicated claim reserve before the recipient can collect. An absent or conflicted professional has independent NPC continuation. Blocking revokes delegated claim or clinical access while preserving the patient's own recovery and reserved reimbursement. Closing insurer sales leaves policy and approved-claim obligations payable. Unused backing cannot be released while a claim or appeal window remains open.

A missing or unusual reference creates a review record, not automatic fraud guilt or confiscation. More complete fraud investigation, courts, sanctions, reinsurance, mass regional disaster exposure and institutional resolution remain within the full build.

Local evidence includes real home deterioration → exposure → game-health change → urgent assessment → single-lease emergency journey → clinical recovery, and real driving wear → bounded breakdown. Tests also cover paid repair and clinical claims, denied preexisting exposure, evidence consent, independent appeal/continuation, reserve exhaustion, payout after insurer closure, retry and backup recovery. Private SQL mirrors enforce reserve and invoice reconciliation and incident-core immutability. PRNG boundary tests control an isolated private seed; players have no seed-edit command. Native browser/device, hosted PostgreSQL/Auth, multiplayer load and full A01–A31 acceptance remain pending.
