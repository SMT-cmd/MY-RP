# Local property and tenancy contract

Build-only components of R017 and R033; rules version property-1.0. All prices and timings are authored balance seeds, not real property values. No requirement or full acceptance scenario is marked complete.

Each representative region currently offers one finite NPC plot, home and warehouse. Land costs 300,000 minor units, a home 500,000 and a warehouse 750,000, plus a disclosed 2.5% title-transfer tax. A purchase reserves the entire total for one minute and either settles title and funds once or refunds. Player title offers preserve disclosed property liabilities and occupied tenancies. Company ownership, mortgages, multiple-unit developments and the complete geography catalogue remain pending.

A tenancy accepts the landlord's published weekly rent and a protected deposit of at most two weeks. The first period pays the owner; the deposit remains reserved. Only the tenant can authorise further personal payments. Offline arrears pause at two periods; outstanding arrears remain recorded after leaving and can be repaid voluntarily. Title changes retain the same tenant and deposit. Joining a home does not transfer personal inventory. Ending a tenancy returns the deposit without an arbitrary damage deduction.

Owner notice requires an evidenced arrears or owner-use ground, seven days, independent factual NPC review and an appeal path before enforcement. Cured arrears invalidate that ground. Enforcement and voluntary departure restore starter accommodation. More complete housing permissions, case-worker adjudication, verified damage deductions and institutional dispute handling remain pending.

Construction consumes five actual material units and two stored power units, reserves 50,000 minor units of NPC labour, and settles after a verified minute. Repair consumes two material units and a disclosed service fee. Condition and fire safety decay at most seven points per resumed offline interval, with a floor of twenty. NPC inspections read measured condition and provide a seven-day repair window without inventing fines.

Connected home services consume actual stored water and energy once daily and record an optional metered service bill, capped at seven uses. Stock acquisition and metered service are distinct costs. Property taxes are disclosed at 50 basis points of the recorded property value per thirty days, with at most two offline periods accrued on return. Bills and taxes cannot automatically drain a citizen account. Alternative supply, outages, business utility connections, vehicle integration and final balance calibration remain pending.

Private PostgreSQL operational mirrors commit with cash, stock, receipts and outbox. Deferred constraints reconcile protected deposits, purchase and construction reserves, unique occupancy and snapshot-to-record consistency. Local embedded PostgreSQL evidence does not establish hosted PostgreSQL compatibility or supported load.
