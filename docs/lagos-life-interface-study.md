# Lagos Life interface inspection

Inspection checkpoint: 12 October 2026. The owner requested direct gameplay research to correct MY-RP's scrolling, text-heavy presentation. This is observed reference evidence and proposed application, not a claim that MY-RP matches another game's quality.

## Observed public interface

The primary reference is https://lagoslife.app/. Its public screen reserves the viewport for the city and overlays compact brand/account controls, Governor, Sea Plots and homes controls. Signup/login is available without navigating through a long dashboard. Public counters were not independently verified and are not performance evidence.

The cloud browser displayed a 3D-start failure and a lite-view message. One retry did not recover rendering. Movement, house entry, interior interaction, furnishing, vehicles and authenticated gameplay have **not** been verified. Account signup is open for the owner; it requires new credentials, date of birth, terms/age confirmation and a human-verification challenge. No account was created automatically, no terms accepted and no challenge attempted.

https://lagoslife.com.ng/ is a separate Naija Life product and must not be presented as the same game. Its public offline option led to avatar/personality/aspiration/home selection. A fictional guest named Reference Visitor selected the default Mushin starter home. The avatar preview reported a WebGL-context error; pressing Move in left the visible home selection unchanged. This inspection did not reach its playable world or validate its advertised screenshots as current gameplay.

## Application to MY-RP

1. Make the graphical city or interior the main playing surface, filling the available screen. Keep identity, needs, balance, location and essential movement/interaction controls compact.
2. Open home, school, work, travel, civic and purchase interfaces when requested or approached. Retain server-authoritative commands and recovery; reorganising forms does not complete the corresponding game systems.
3. Keep the adult avatar readable during camera pan, zoom and turns. Verify actual movement and object contact on supported devices rather than using marketing descriptions as acceptance evidence.
4. Provide a graphical low-data fallback with direct object selection where Canvas is supported. Preserve accessible controls for environments without graphics support.
5. Inspect authenticated doors, rooms, furniture placement, walking/camera controls and phone menus after the owner's demo signup. Record observed behaviour separately from design inference.

No proprietary source, character models, textures, animations or maps were copied. Public UI observations establish interaction references, not the competitor's engine architecture.
